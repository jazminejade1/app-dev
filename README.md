# Movie Review: Moana

![Moana](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/2/26/Moana_Teaser_Poster.jpg)

## About the Movie

**Moana** is a Disney animated adventure movie released in 2016. It tells the story of a young girl who travels across the ocean to save her people and discover her true identity.

## Movie Details

| Information | Details |
|---|---|
| Movie Title | Moana |
| Release Date | November 23, 2016 |
| Genre | Animation, Adventure, Musical |
| Directors | Ron Clements and John Musker |
| Production | Walt Disney Animation Studios |
| Rating | 10/10 |

## My Review

For me, Moana is an amazing movie because it teaches us to be brave and never give up. I also love the animation, music, and beautiful ocean scenes. The story is entertaining and has a good message about family and believing in yourself.

## Characters

- **Moana** – A brave girl who wants to save her island.
- **Maui** – A funny and powerful demigod.
- **Gramma Tala** – Moana's grandmother who supports her journey.
- **Chief Tui** – Moana's father and the leader of their island.

## My Top 3 Favorite Things

1. The beautiful ocean animation.
2. The songs, especially *How Far I'll Go*.
3. The friendship between Moana and Maui.

## Favorite Quote

> "See the line where the sky meets the sea? It calls me."
